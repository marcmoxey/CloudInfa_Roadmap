**How to choose AWS Region?**
	**Compliance** with data governance & legal requirements
		- never leaves region without explicit permission
	**Proximity** to customers 
		- reduced latency
	**Available Services with in Region**
		- new services & features aren't available in every region
	**Pricing**
		- varies region to region 

**AWS Availability Zone**
	- Ex:  ap-southeast-2a, ap-southeast-2b, ap-southeast-2c
	-  usually 3 zones(min) max is 6 
	- Each zone is one or more discrete data center with redundant power, networking &  connectivity 
	- Connected with high bandwidth, ultra-low latency networking 

**AWS Points if Presence (Edge Location)**
	- 400+ points in 90+ cities across 40 + countries
