

**What is a Networking?**
- Networks are things connected; how devices communicate with each other 

**What is the Internet?**
-  Small networks all joined together
	-  private & public network

**Identifying Devices on a Network**
- IP Address (Internet Protocol)
	- Can also be associated with another device without IP address changing. but not at the same time
	- -divide in to 4 octets 
		- Ex 192.168.1.1
	- Public address is used to identify device on the internet 
	- Private is used to identify device amongst other devices
- Media Access Control (MAC) Address
	-  Fake or spoofed
	- globally unique 

**Ping (ICMP)**
- Internet Control Message Protocol
	-  Determine performance by sending packets
